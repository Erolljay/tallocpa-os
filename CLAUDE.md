# CLAUDE.md — Tallo CPA Tax Compliance Workflow

## Project context
- Firm: Tallo CPA (EJ Tallo Cloud Accounting and Auditing Service), Iloilo City, Philippines
- Goal: automate BIR tax compliance from client document intake to filing, payment, and portal close-out
- Stack: Client portal, Supabase (books/data layer; Google Sheets only as an optional export), Google Drive (per-client/per-year folders), n8n (orchestration + all tax computation), Gemini parser (document extraction), Claude (AI categorization), Moxie CRM (billing), UnionBank app (BIR payments), BIR eFPS/eBIRForms (actual filing)

## Hard rules
1. **No AI computes tax.** All tax computations are deterministic n8n workflows using a rates/thresholds settings table. AI is limited to categorization and parsing.
2. **Taxable-year boundary.** Held (deferred) sales/purchases may move between periods but MUST be reported within the same taxable year. Year close is blocked while the hold bucket contains items for that year.
3. **Deferred sales require practice-lead sign-off** with a logged reason (late 2550Q exposure). Deferred purchases get aging alerts.
4. **Client funds are not firm income.** Tax money received from clients is recorded as a liability ("Client funds for remittance") and cleared on confirmed BIR payment.
5. BIR forms generated by the system are **pro forma only**; official filing is done in BIR systems.

## Workflow

### 1. Upload
- Client uploads source documents (invoices, ORs, receipts) to the portal.
- Store in Drive: `/{Client}/{Year}/Intake/`.

### 2. AI categorization
- Classify each document: `sales` | `purchases` | `expenses`.
- Tag tax validity: `valid_vat_and_it` | `valid_it_only` | `not_valid`.

### 3. Parsing (n8n + Gemini parser)
Extract per document:
- Counterparty name, TIN, registered address
- Invoice/OR number and date
- Gross amount, VATable, VAT-exempt, zero-rated amounts
- Input/output VAT
- Withholding tax amount and ATC

### 4. Review bucket
- All parsed items land in the bucket.
- Auto-flag failed validations:
  - Missing/invalid TIN format
  - VAT amount does not compute (12% of VATable base)
  - Duplicate invoice number per counterparty
  - Missing required fields
- Flagged items reviewed by staff or practice lead.
- Review notes are stored and carried forward to the client view.
- Clients may request to **hold** items for a later period (see Hard rules 2–3).

### 5. Period assignment and posting
- Once cleared, assign each entry to month/quarter.
- Post to the client's books (Supabase: status `posted`, view `client_books`).

### 6. Tax computation (n8n, deterministic)
- Pull posted period figures.
- Compute: output VAT − input VAT (incl. excess input VAT carryover), EWT/compensation withholding totals, income tax.
- Rates and thresholds read from settings table only.

### 7. Internal check, then client sign-off
- **Variance check:** flag if tax due differs materially from prior period; firm reviews before release.
- **Client portal view:**
  - Reported this period (items + review notes)
  - Why tax is payable (plain explanation, e.g. "Output VAT ₱X − Input VAT ₱Y = ₱Z")
  - Carried over (held items, excess input VAT, deadline within taxable year)
- Client signs off.
- Sign-off triggers Moxie invoice for professional fee.

### 8. Forms and DAT files
- Generate pro forma BIR forms (2550Q, 1601-C, 0619-E, 1601-EQ, 1701Q/1702Q, etc.).
- Generate DAT files: SLS, SLP, QAP, SAWT.
- Firm files through BIR systems.

### 9. Payment
- Client pays firm: tax amount + professional fee.
- Record tax portion as liability "Client funds for remittance."
- Firm pays BIR via UnionBank app.

### 10. Close-out
- Firm uploads filed returns and proof of payment to client portal.
- Match payment to return by reference number and amount; unmatched/short → back to review bucket.
- Post payments to client books: clear VAT payable, income tax payable, withholding payable, PhilHealth/SSS/Pag-IBIG liabilities.
- Clear "Client funds for remittance" on confirmed payment.

## Status values (suggested)
`uploaded → categorized → parsed → in_review → flagged | held | cleared → posted → computed → variance_checked → client_signed_off → filed → client_paid → bir_paid → closed`

In the database (`review_status`), `needs_review` = `in_review` and `approved` = `cleared`; the other names match.
